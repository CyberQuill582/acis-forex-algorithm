**Four of four families that reached the 2024–25 holdout earned nothing there.** Three structural reasons:
1. **Provenance (§5.6).** Every family was selected by an LLM from published work, so history can only kill it, and credit comes only from forward data. No amount of history testing can certify one.
2. **Decay.** Published effects lose about half their return after publication (McLean & Pontiff 2016). The holdout shows the rest of the fade.
3. **Venue friction.** The CFD round trip (2–4 bp) eats every effect that trades more than about weekly (IMOM, GOLD-EW). A futures venue fixes cost, but the prop firms that host futures exclude a Kenyan, cloud-run bot (`archive/RESEARCH_LEVER2_FUTURES_2026-10-03.md`).

## 2. What "winning" realistically is (GAP-P12D-148, verified)

- The ruled target is **~0.2% of the account a week**, judged over 6–12 months. It needs a real net Sharpe of **~0.65–0.85**.
- Live practitioner records sit at **~0.3–0.8**. Bank-built strategies went from 1.20 in backtest to 0.31 live.
- Prop challenge economics (PROPRETURNS, re-run on 20,000 paths): with the 8% drawdown option, the pass probability is 0.27 / 0.43 / 0.62 at a Sharpe of 0 / 0.5 / 1.0, for an expected value per $10k of −$57 / +$33 / +$224. **At D1's measured 0.16, the expected value is negative in every cell.** A paid challenge loses money until a forward Sharpe of about 0.3–0.5 is shown.
