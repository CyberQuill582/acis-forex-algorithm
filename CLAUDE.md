## Adversarial audits by subordinate models (deploy-and-continue) — TWO PER PHASE, BINDING
**Owner directives (2026-07-15; hardened 2026-07-16 after the external review): every completed phase gets TWO parallel Opus 4.8 adversarial audits, both reports are logged, and all findings are acted upon.** The cycle is constitutional: at each phase's completion, launch **two background Opus 4.8 subagents in parallel**:
1. **The internal-consistency audit** (the original): *assume the work is wrong; construct the failing input; hunt flaws, self-contradiction, weakness, regression, and drift from `ACIS_SPEC.md`.* Spec-vs-code, reproduced with scripts.
2. **The external-referent audit** (added 2026-07-16 — the internal audit's proven ceiling is that it can only show ACIS is what ACIS *says* it is): *compare the implementation AND the spec against their EXTERNAL referents — the cited literature and the standard definitions of every indicator/statistic.* Binding questions: for every module citing a source, does the implementation match the source's **actual method**, and is every departure recorded in its `declared_deviations`? Does every named indicator (ATR, ADX, EMA, correlation, Hurst, …) compute what its name means, on the time base its definition assumes? An undeclared deviation is a finding; **a spec that is itself wrong about the outside world is a finding** — the code faithfully implementing it is no defense. 3. **The operational-referent pass (standing, from go-live — owner directive 2026-07-18).** The external-referent lens does not stop at launch; the spec-is-wrong-about-the-world risk *resurfaces* the moment real fills exist. On a defined cadence once trading is live, re-run the external-referent audit against **live behaviour, not just the spec**: does the modelled cost/carry/slippage/fill still match what the broker actually did (GAP-0001)? Does the financing table match real swap charges? Do reconnects, weekend gaps, and the **observed live VaR breach frequency vs the 1% level** behave as modelled? This is the one check neither a behavioural log nor an internal audit catches alone — the internal audit shares the author's priors (the system is co-designed; that shared-premise seam is the standing risk), and a log only records a surprise after it surfaces. This pass reads the **operational ledger** (`OPERATIONS_LEDGER.md`) and the live logs as its evidence, and triages exactly like a phase audit. Never let the external-referent lens (phase OR operational) collapse into a generic "review this" pass — its whole value is that it is designed to fail *differently* from the internal one.

## Operational correctness — three independent guards (from the first live fill)
Build correctness (the phase audits) is not operational correctness, and the gap opens the day this trades real money. Correctness then rests on **three independent guards; none substitutes for another, and treating "just log it" as the whole is the failure mode**:
1. **The two-lens per-phase audit stays exactly as it is.** Do not downgrade the external-referent lens. The two lenses earn their keep by failing differently; the external-referent one is the only structural attack on the shared-priors seam of a co-designed system.
2. **Real out-of-sample data + human sign-off before live — separate from auditing, non-negotiable.** Auditing the harness proves the *harness* is faithful; it says nothing about whether the *strategy* earns money on data it has not seen. Never present in-sample numbers against §14 (rule 6). The human owns the architecture and business-logic calls; the model chain is never terminal authority there.
3. **Runtime ledger discipline from the day it trades real money.** Broker quirks, real-vs-modelled fills (GAP-0001), financing-table reality, reconnect/weekend-gap behaviour, observed live VaR breach frequency — the new "tribal knowledge." Log each in `OPERATIONS_LEDGER.md` with the **same Severity/Likelihood/Status/Justification structure** as `KNOWN_GAPS.md`, so both audit lenses stay effective instead of blind. But a log captures surprises *after* they surface — it is never a substitute for guard 2. Do not let confidence from clean internal audits substitute for guard 1's second lens or for guard 2.
