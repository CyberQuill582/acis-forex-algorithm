## Operational correctness — three independent guards (from the first live fill)
Build correctness (the phase audits) is not operational correctness, and the gap opens the day this trades real money. Correctness then rests on **three independent guards; none substitutes for another, and treating "just log it" as the whole is the failure mode**:
1. **The two-lens per-phase audit stays exactly as it is.** Do not downgrade the external-referent lens. The two lenses earn their keep by failing differently; the external-referent one is the only structural attack on the shared-priors seam of a co-designed system.
2. **Real out-of-sample data + human sign-off before live — separate from auditing, non-negotiable.** Auditing the harness proves the *harness* is faithful; it says nothing about whether the *strategy* earns money on data it has not seen. Never present in-sample numbers against §14 (rule 6). The human owns the architecture and business-logic calls; the model chain is never terminal authority there.
3. **Runtime ledger discipline from the day it trades real money.** Broker quirks, real-vs-modelled fills (GAP-0001), financing-table reality, reconnect/weekend-gap behaviour, observed live VaR breach frequency — the new "tribal knowledge." Log each in `OPERATIONS_LEDGER.md` with the **same Severity/Likelihood/Status/Justification structure** as `KNOWN_GAPS.md`, so both audit lenses stay effective instead of blind. But a log captures surprises *after* they surface — it is never a substitute for guard 2. Do not let confidence from clean internal audits substitute for guard 1's second lens or for guard 2.
